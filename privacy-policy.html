At Fantasy Shop, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, and address.
  • Account Data: Login credentials and preferences.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, products viewed, and interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders and deliver products.
  • To personalize your shopping experience.
  • To improve website performance and usability.
  • To communicate updates, offers, and support.

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Your Rights

  • Access and update your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications.

5. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@fantasyshop.example.
