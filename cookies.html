Effective Date: 07/09/2023

This Cookies Policy explains how Fantasy Shop uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions, preferences, and browsing behavior over time.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to support essential site functionality, improve performance, understand how visitors use our site, and personalize your shopping experience.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core website functionality.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the site.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your choices and settings.
  • Marketing Cookies: Help deliver relevant promotions and product recommendations.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect parts of the shopping experience.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookies Policy, contact us at support@fantasyshop.example.
